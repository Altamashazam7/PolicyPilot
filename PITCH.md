# PolicyPilot

## Problem

Managing DeFi portfolios is difficult.

Users must:

- Track allocations manually
- Monitor portfolio drift
- Evaluate risk
- Decide when to rebalance
- Execute trades

Most users do not have the time or expertise required.

---

## Solution

PolicyPilot allows users to define investment policies in plain English.

Example:

"Keep 40% USDC and 60% SOL"

PolicyPilot:

1. Parses the policy
2. Evaluates compliance
3. Assesses risk
4. Generates recommendations
5. Explains reasoning
6. Previews trades
